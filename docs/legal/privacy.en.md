# Privacy policy

Last updated: {{date}}

This policy explains what happens to your data when you visit naice-notes.com and when you use the
Naice Notes web app. A German version is available; if the two differ, the German version prevails.

## The short version

- Naice Notes is a free, invite only notes app run by one person. There are no ads, no analytics,
  no tracking and no cookies.
- Your notes are stored on your device and synced through a database in Frankfurt, Germany.
- When you scan a recipe, the photo goes to an AI model and back. It isn't stored and isn't used for
  training.
- If you share a list, the people you share it with see its content and your email address.
- You can ask me at any time to see, export or delete your data.

## 2. Visiting the website and the app

The website and the web app are delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco,
CA 94107, USA). Every time a page is requested, Cloudflare processes technical data that your
browser sends automatically: IP address, date and time, the requested address, the referrer,
browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the pages, to keep them secure and to
fend off attacks.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is a secure, working website.
- **Storage:** Cloudflare keeps this data only briefly for security and operation. I don't
  evaluate it and don't combine it with other data.
- I have a data processing agreement with Cloudflare. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data
  Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

The website doesn't load fonts, scripts or images from third parties.

## 3. Storage on your device

The app stores the following in your browser's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB):

- your sign in session, so you stay signed in,
- a copy of your lists and notes, so the app works offline,
- changes that haven't been synced yet,
- display settings, such as the last list you opened.

This storage is strictly necessary to provide the app you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), so it
doesn't need your consent. Nothing is used for tracking or advertising. Signing out removes the
session and the local copy of your notes from the device.

## 4. Account and sign in

Naice Notes has no passwords. You enter your email address and type the six digit code I send you.
To do this I process your email address, the time of sign up and sign in, and the IP address used
for the request (to protect against abuse). Only addresses that have been invited can create an
account.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app you signed up for) and, for the IP address,
  Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (protection against abuse).
- **Service provider:** accounts are managed by Supabase, Inc. (USA), see section 5.
- **Emails:** sign in codes and invitations are sent through a Google Mail account. For this,
  Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) processes the recipient's
  address and the content of the email. Google LLC (USA) is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy
  Framework.

## 5. Your notes and syncing

Your lists and notes (text, sort order, due dates and repeats, links, timestamps) are synced
between your devices through a database run by Supabase. The database is hosted in the EU
(Frankfurt, Germany, on Amazon Web Services). Supabase cannot rule out that its staff access data
from outside the EU for support and operation; for this I have a data processing agreement with
Supabase that includes the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Supabase also keeps technical logs (for example IP address and time of a request) for a short
  time, to operate the service.

Please don't use Naice Notes for special categories of personal data (such as health data) or
for other people's sensitive data.

## 6. Shared lists and invitations

You can share a single list with other people by entering their email address. Then:

- I store the invited address and send that person one email that says who shared which list with
  them and how to sign in.
- Everyone in a shared list sees the list's content and the email addresses of the other members
  and open invitations.
- Changes appear for everyone in the list.

**If you were invited:** someone who uses Naice Notes gave me your email address. I use it only to
send you the invitation and to let you sign in. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate
interest is enabling the person who invited you to share a list with you. If you don't sign in,
nothing else happens. You can object at any time by writing to {{email}}, and I will delete your
address.

**Storage:** an invitation is kept until it is accepted or until the list is unshared or deleted.

## 7. Recipe scan (AI)

If you scan a recipe, the app shrinks the photo and sends it to my server function, which asks an
AI model to read the ingredients off it. The ingredients come back to your app, where you check
them before anything is added.

- **What is sent:** only the photo. Make sure it doesn't show people or other personal details.
- **Who processes it:** OpenRouter, Inc. (USA) forwards the request to the model, currently
  Google's Gemini on Google Cloud Vertex AI (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). OpenRouter is set
  to zero data retention and requests are sent with data collection denied. Google may keep
  requests for a limited time to detect abuse, but doesn't use them for training.
- **What I keep:** nothing from the photo or the ingredients. My server only logs technical data
  (model, number of tokens, success or failure).
- **Transfers:** data is transferred to the USA. My data processing agreement with OpenRouter
  includes the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR); Google LLC is certified under
  the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The scan only runs when you start it.

The ingredients are generated by an AI and can be wrong or incomplete. That's why you check them
before they are added. The AI doesn't make any decisions about you (Art. 22 GDPR).

## 8. How long data is kept

- Your account, lists and notes: until you delete your account.
- Deleted notes and lists: removed from the server at most 31 days after you delete them. Until then they
  are kept so that all your devices can learn about the deletion.
- Invitations: see section 6.
- Logs at Cloudflare and Supabase: a few days at most, see sections 2 and 5.
- Data I am legally required to keep: as long as the law requires.

To delete your account, write to {{email}} from the address you use to sign in. I will delete your
account and your notes within 30 days. Lists you shared with others are deleted as well, also for
the other members. If you are a member of someone else's list, that list stays with its owner.
